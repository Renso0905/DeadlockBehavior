# Ground Soul Lifecycle Extended A V01

Promotes three narrowly defined replay-generic Ground Soul lifecycle metrics to Extended A:

- `ground_soul_activations`
- `ground_soul_targeted_activations`
- `ground_soul_lifecycle_duration`

Authority is restricted to the replicated `CCitadel_Pickup_AssignedGold` lifecycle carrier: `m_bActive` activation/termination and physical `m_hVacuumTarget` acquisition. It does **not** promote economic recipient identity, collector identity, last hitter identity, exact vacuum radius, reward value/formula, or unmatched-death interpretations.

Expected authority ledger after successful production: Core A 68/68, Extended A 5/5, Total A 73/73.
