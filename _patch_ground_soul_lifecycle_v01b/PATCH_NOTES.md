# Ground Soul Lifecycle Extended A V01b

## Repair purpose
V01 could stop after partially applying the Ground Soul promotion because the installer itself interpolated `${id}` while constructing the production-pipeline regression test. V01b escapes that template expression and is intentionally idempotent against the partially installed V01 state.

## What remains unchanged
The scientific/semantic scope is unchanged: Ground Soul activations, physical vacuum-targeted activations, and completed active-lifecycle duration only. Vacuum target is not economic recipient/collector/last hitter; inactive is not payout/expiration; no exact 732/735-HU radius or reward formula is promoted.
